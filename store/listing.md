## Description

Test your site on every screen size at once, and see what breaks.

Samestate opens a page on a wall of phones, tablets and desktops side by side, and keeps them in the same state. Click Products on the desktop and the phone opens its hamburger menu first, then Products. Scroll, type, open a dialog or change tabs on any screen, and every other screen follows, even when the layouts are completely different.

Then it measures every device against the desktop and tells you what broke:
• Pages that scroll sideways, and the element that pushes them out
• Tables wider than their container, and text that is cut off
• Tap targets under 24px and 40px, and text under 12px
• Broken and stretched images, missing viewport tags
• Content and navigation missing on one screen but shown on another
• Accessibility, metadata, social card and performance findings
Each problem lists the screens it affects and the CSS that usually fixes it. Preview the fix on every screen before you change your code.

WHAT ELSE IS IN IT
• Run all checks: grouped automated checks, a 320–1920px width sweep and human review, exported as HTML or JSON
• Outline and measurements: headings, landmarks and focus order, with CSS sizes, spacing, type and contrast for any element
• Debug tools and 20 visual simulators: layout outlines, contrast, target size, color blindness, low vision and more, on one screen or all of them
• Meta and console: titles, descriptions and seven social card previews; console messages from every screen in one place
• Breakpoints, find elements and a ⌘K command bar
• Compare two websites side by side, or the steps of one journey
• Screenshots, full-page captures and recordings of the device wall
• Test runs: record a journey once and replay it on every screen
• 83 device presets, plus your own, with phone and tablet identity
• A 46-step narrated demo, and voice help on every control

ALSO FOR AI CODING
The same checks work from Claude Code: the Samestate plugin lets Claude open your page on every screen size, read the report with the exact CSS selector of each element, look at screenshots, fix the layout and check again. It runs on your computer and works with localhost. Install it with /plugin marketplace add shouvik12/samestate.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Samestate has no server and no account. Pages load straight from the site into the device wall in your browser, and settings stay in your browser's storage. Chrome asks before Samestate opens a new site. Privacy policy: https://shouvik12.github.io/samestate/privacy.html

Free and open source (Apache 2.0): https://github.com/shouvik12/samestate
Try the live demo first: https://shouvik12.github.io/samestate/
